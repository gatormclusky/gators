# Takur Ghar, aftermath imagery requests (after 4 Mar 2002)

Target: peak at about 33.34 N, 69.21 E (approximate, sources differ in the 4th decimal).
Search box: `search_area.geojson` (69.15-69.28 E, 33.30-33.40 N, about 12 x 11 km).
Window: 2002-03-05 to 2003-12-31. Anything after the battle is useful, so take earliest first.

Free baseline (30 m, no detail): `landsat_scenes_2002.csv`. Best are 2002-03-05/06/13/14.
Scene cloud cover is for the whole scene, not the box; check the preview.

## 1. Archive search, QuickBird and IKONOS (Vantor / reseller)

Send to Vantor Hub sales or a reseller such as SkyFi or LAND INFO. A footprint list is free;
buy only passes that cover the peak and ridge.

> I need an archive search (footprints, capture dates, off-nadir angle, cloud cover, resolution)
> for QuickBird and IKONOS over this AOI, attached as GeoJSON: Takur Ghar, Paktia Province,
> Afghanistan, about 33.34 N 69.21 E, in a 12 x 11 km box. Date range 2002-03-05 to 2003-12-31.
> Please include pansharpened product options, any "stereo" or multi-date coverage, price per km2
> for archive delivery, minimum order area, and license terms for publication in a research or
> journalism project. Is the AOI covered by any IKONOS data from the Space Imaging / GeoEye archive?

## 2. USGS EROS, publicly available IKONOS-2

USGS holds licensed IKONOS imagery searchable in EarthExplorer. Most is restricted to
federal users by vendor license, but a subset is released publicly
(https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:EROS5e83d023ae36bbed). Search EarthExplorer
(Commercial Satellites > IKONOS) with the same box and dates, and see which results you may
order. Confirm the current contact address on the USGS page before emailing.

## 3. Primary-source detail imagery

The Army's Operation Enduring Freedom history lists a "Drone Image of Takur Ghar, 4 March 2002"
among its figures (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D114-PURL-gpo194477/pdf/GOVPUB-D114-PURL-gpo194477.pdf).
Ask the U.S. Army Center of Military History for the original and its release status.

## Record for every image you use

Sensor, capture date/time (UTC), off-nadir angle, resolution, cloud/snow cover, source and license.
