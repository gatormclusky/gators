Sequence, 4 March 2002

  1. 02:45 The first MH-47 (Razor 03, MAKO 30) nears the summit; an RPG hits it, Neil Roberts falls out, the pilot aborts. sun 44° below horizon
  2. 04:57 MAKO 30 lands again to rescue him; Chapman charges a bunker, Roberts is found dead, the team pulls back and calls in an AC-130. sun 18° below
  3. ~05:20 Drone footage shows Chapman recovering and fighting on alone for about 45 minutes. sun 13° below
  4. 06:05 A Ranger MH-47 (Razor 01, Capt. Self) lands under fire and crash-lands; three Rangers are mortally wounded leaving it. sun 3.5° below, civil twilight
  5. A second MH-47 puts Canon's 13 Rangers (Chalk 2) 600 m down the slope; they climb for 2.5+ hours under 82 mm mortar fire.
  6. F-16s and Predator Hellfires disable the bunkers; the peak is cleared by 11:30. Several counterattacks follow; extraction is after nightfall.
Times are as printed in the Army history (Folse 2022, pp. 60-62), which doesn't state a time zone; I've assumed local (UTC+4:30). Detail from Copeland et al. 2021. 7 Americans died (1 SEAL, 2 Air Force, 4 Army); the Army counts the summit as about 3,160 m.
