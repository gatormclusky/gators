Sequence (Copeland et al. 2021)

  1. Night of 3-4 Mar: an AC-130 infrared sweep declares the summit landing zone (LZ) "cold".
  2. Razor 03 (MAKO 30, SEAL team) flares to land on the summit and is hit by an RPG. Neil Roberts falls out; the aircraft makes a controlled crash away from the peak.
  3. Razor 04 recovers the crew to Gardez, then reinserts MAKO 30 at the same LZ under heavy fire. Roberts is not found and the team breaks contact.
  4. Ranger quick reaction force from Bagram: Razor 01 (Chalk 1) lands at the same LZ and crash-lands under RPG fire.
  5. Razor 02 (Chalk 2) lands over 600 m below the summit; the Rangers climb a northeast-facing slope for 2.5+ hours to reach Chalk 1.
  6. Strafing runs and a Predator Hellfire on the main bunker; the Rangers take the summit and wait for dark to exfiltrate.
17-hour fight; 7 US killed, 2 MH-47E lost.
